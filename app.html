PanSwap

The recipe says 8-inch round and you own a 9x13. Scale every ingredient by pan area.

About this

The conversion factor is the area of the pan you have divided by the area of the pan the recipe uses, then every ingredient is multiplied by it. Matches the chart from America's Test Kitchen (27 of its 30 cells exactly; into a 6-inch pan from 9-inch round, 8x8 or 9x9 it rounds 0.1 higher than the raw ratio). Keeping the same depth keeps the bake time about the same, but check early and use a toothpick. Fill pans about half to two-thirds full. Baking is chemistry, so odd ratios like 0.4 can be fiddly.
